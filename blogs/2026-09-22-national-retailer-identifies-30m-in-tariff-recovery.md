Introduction When the U.S. Supreme Court struck down the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA) tariffs in February 2026, it unlocked an estimated $175 billion in potential refunds. However, the refunds flow directly to the importer of record, meaning the supplier receives those funds and not the customer who paid the higher price.
